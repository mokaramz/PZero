## A. Executive summary

Project Zero can already produce real findings, but a **zero result is currently ambiguous**. Scouts 01–07 are operationally strong on **DE / CH / FR / UK** (formation, named national/EU grant programs, DACH events, English funding media). They are **not** systematically covering most of the rest of Europe’s early-stage surfaces. Estonia / Tallinn–Tartu is the clearest proof: a dense, public, early-signal ecosystem that current routing would mostly miss until English seed+ press or DIANA/ESA/EIC spillover.

**Coverage health (our vision, not ecosystem quality):**
- **HEALTHY:** Germany, Switzerland, France, United Kingdom (core hunt geos) — with residual regional gaps.
- **PARTIAL:** Ireland, Netherlands, Nordics, Austria (media/DIANA/event spillover; not systematic).
- **GAP:** Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Czechia, Portugal, Spain, Italy (as intentional early-stage watches).
- **UNKNOWN:** ecosystems not yet sampled with falsifiable watchers.

**Implication:** Do not “solve Estonia by adding Estonia” alone. Build a **coverage registry + Chief Scout coverage-health labels** so zero findings can be reported as `ZERO FINDINGS — COVERAGE HEALTHY` vs `ZERO FINDINGS — INSUFFICIENT COVERAGE`, and repeated unexpected geos trigger `NEW ECOSYSTEM — COVERAGE REVIEW`.

**This audit delivers:** scout-by-scout vision map, EU matrix, 49 public source-pack candidates (Estonia-heavy acceptance case), coverage-health rules (additive only), and a machine-readable registry candidate JSON. **No methods or schedules were changed.**

## F. Chief Scout coverage-health rules (additive only)

Do **not** rewrite Scout methodologies. Add labels at synthesis / Executive Brief / intake-packet metadata level only.

### F1. Zero-finding interpretation
After each Full Cycle or Midweek Pulse, for each Scout (and optionally per ecosystem in scope):

1. **`ZERO FINDINGS — COVERAGE HEALTHY`**  
   Use when: (a) the Scout’s **declared hunt geos** were actively searched with its **normal source families**, and (b) coverage registry marks those geos/families **HEALTHY** or **PARTIAL with watchers executed this cycle**, and (c) no tooling/budget failure blocked the hunt.

2. **`ZERO FINDINGS — INSUFFICIENT COVERAGE`**  
   Use when: (a) the question implied an ecosystem/family **outside HEALTHY watches**, or (b) required watchers were not run / failed / budget-exhausted before geo sampling, or (c) registry marks the target geo/family as **GAP** or **UNKNOWN**.

3. Never upgrade a zero to a positive finding. Never manufacture entities to avoid zeros.

### F2. `NEW ECOSYSTEM — COVERAGE REVIEW`
Scout 00 flags this (human decision; no auto expansion) when **any** of:

- ≥2 credible signals in ≤8 weeks from the **same ecosystem** that is **GAP/UNKNOWN** in the registry and was **not** in the hunt allowlist;
- ≥1 exceptional founder/company from a GAP ecosystem passes bar via spillover **and** local public surfaces exist in the registry candidate list;
- Mohamed explicitly asks about a country/city not yet HEALTHY.

Flag content: ecosystem · signal families seen · registry status · proposed watcher pack (from registry) · **no** auto contact / RSVP / investment.

### F3. Registry hygiene
- Coverage status = **our vision**, not ecosystem ranking.
- Prefer public official/TTO/grant/event sources.
- UNKNOWN is allowed; do not invent HEALTHY.
- Expanding watchers is a **Mohamed approval** change, separate from frozen Scout methods.
