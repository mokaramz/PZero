# PROJECT ZERO v4 — weekly routine (~2 hours)

> **New to Project Zero v1?** Start with the [operator guide](OPERATOR_GUIDE.md): daily / Monday / midweek routines, founder calls, validation and backups.

**Monday**
1. `python dashboard.py` → Discover → Find New Candidates (10 min).
2. Work the **Pre-company only** filter first — that's the edge. Announcement feeds second.
3. Manual sources (30 min): LinkedIn stealth watchlist (Helsing/Quantum/ARX/STARK alumni; Siemens, Bosch, TRUMPF, DB Schenker, DHL, Allianz, Munich Re, BASF seniors; ex-McKinsey/BCG industrial & defence), Project A Resilience newsletter jobs board, one transfer-office message.
4. Research (10 min per candidate): who (founders), when (fundraising timing), conflict (portfolio). Keep only with evidence.
5. Score honestly. Tick verification boxes only after checking. Watch Provisional vs Verified.

**Tuesday–Thursday — act by timing, not by freshness**
- Raising now / 0–6 m → contact this week (email drafted per timing).
- 6–12 m / recently funded → relationship mode: congratulate, learn, calendar a check-in. Do not pitch a round.
- Potential portfolio conflict → do NOT contact as a deal; ask Omar.

**Friday**
Generate Radar → send `weekly_memo.html` + best memo to Omar with one question: *"Which of these would you take a call with, and why?"* — and the standing conflict questions (Aereus vs Project Q, RegCheck vs Rematiq, a68 vs Nettle).

**Rules**
- Archetypes are hypotheses; #15 outlier is open to anything with an exceptional team, huge market, powerful why-now, real differentiation, early.
- Similarity ≠ positive. Conflict check is mandatory.
- Empty Radar slots are correct. 3 real Project-A-quality companies a month > 300 tech-sounding GmbHs.

**Focus architecture — where things moved (nothing was removed)**
- Every screen shows what needs you first; the selected item's context opens on the right; history, provenance and machine detail are folded. Only one folded section per area is open at a time.
- Deal Flow: the four everyday filters stay visible; Researching / Watch / Kept / Rejected, Machine triage, Agent Review Queue, Baseline, the theme and the Investment manager lens are under **More filters** (the active one is named on the button). *Find New Candidates* / *Analyze Top* and the scan report sit below the list.
- Today: the date and a small flow (your missions → decisions due → machine handled) at the top, then the briefing, four tiles, the *Missions* list (the first is the one to do now), and *Manager decisions* beside *Events*; machine work is folded at the bottom. Scout Intake: *Scout 00 intake* and *Native Scout* tabs (`/scout-intake?tab=native`); Monitor / Archive / Mark reviewing are under **More** in the rail, Feedback / note is folded; inbox, transport and cycle accounting sit below the queue.
- Validation: one question at a time — Are we early? · Are we right? · Which sources work? · Where do we miss? · What does it cost?; *Record a miss* is under **More**. System health: Action required · External limitations · Healthy details (folded). Radar: the method is folded; empty slots show as one line. Network: Identity review is one row, extra segments under More filters.
- **Dark mode**: the sun / moon switch in the top bar; your choice is remembered on this computer.
- **Focus mode**: press **F** (or *Focus* in the top bar) to hide the navigation; press again to bring it back. Remembered on this computer.

**Discover — scan freshness & feedback (Company Discovery Freshness Release)**
- Every *Find New Candidates* run writes a scan ledger (Discover → under the source summary, and `/api/scan_history`): sources attempted / successful / failed, raw records retrieved, genuinely new companies, already-known skipped, duplicates merged, baseline records, scan timestamp, and for each new company its source URL, source date and first-seen date. An unchanged source yields zero genuinely new; a source that adds one company surfaces exactly that company.
- The nine bundled Project A Radar #01 companies are **Existing pipeline — not discovered in this scan**. They are never "new this run" and a scan never re-adds a pipeline company to the inbox.
- **Feedback-adjusted sourcing priority**: your Keep / Research / Reject decisions and Omar's explicit + / − outcome adjust the *ordering* of future candidates from the same source, and nothing else. Neutral prior; a source is adjusted only after five unique reviewed companies (Keep + Omar outcome on one company count once; a replay or changed outcome replaces the earlier state); capped at −3…+3; the adjustment and its reason appear in every candidate's ranking explanation next to Project A fit, source quality, freshness and cross-source confirmation. It never stops fetching, never disables a source, never rejects, hides or deletes a company. No model is trained. The Taste Map is unchanged.

**Pre-GO RC1**
- *Scout Bridge* (Sourcing rail): **New research job** creates a structured job for Scout 00 — `COVERAGE_RESEARCH`, `COMPANY_RESEARCH`, `VERIFY_CLAIM`, `FOUNDER_IDENTITY_RESEARCH`, `IMQ_BLOCKER_RESEARCH`, `SOURCE_REPAIR_RESEARCH`, `SCOUT_PACKET`. Jobs are written to `project-zero-data/bridge/outbox/<job>.json` (and POSTed to a Scout 00 endpoint when `config.json` has `"scout_bridge": {"endpoint": "…"}`); **Copy job JSON** hands the job to Scout 00 by hand. Responses come back via **Paste Scout 00 response**, a JSON file dropped into `project-zero-data/bridge/inbox/`, or `POST /api/bridge_response`. Every response is validated (job id, allowed type, payload shape, URLs, provenance) before anything is staged; an invalid one is **FAILED** with the reason (raw text kept) and nothing changes. Tabs: OUTBOX · RUNNING · INBOX · NEEDS MOHAMED · FAILED · CLOSED. What Scout 00 may do automatically: research, transport, stage. What stays yours: candidate sources become watchers only via **Adopt as watcher** (Ecosystems); claim evidence attaches only via **Attach as public source** (Active Evaluator); identity evidence lands unconfirmed (Founder Radar → Confirm identity); a dated why-now source is recorded only via **Record as dated why-now trigger**; packets land in Scout Intake staging under the existing approval, duplicate and identity rules. Short-cuts: *ask Scout 00* on a GAP family (Ecosystems), on an IMQ timing blocker (Conviction), on a failing source (Sources), *verify via Scout 00* on a ledger claim (Active Evaluator), *Ask Scout 00 for identity evidence* on an unresolved person (Founder Radar).
- *Active Evaluator*: contradictions now also cover counted things (deck "18 paying customers" vs founder "12 paying customers" → one CONTRADICTION linking both claims; restating it does not add a second one); they show live under **Contradictions**, in the ended summary and in the Founder Call observations. Ending an evaluation maps founder-reported answers into the six canonical Founder Call sections (why · who · built · pull · scale · team) where the rubric family fits — uncovered sections stay UNKNOWN, nothing is invented, provenance stays *Founder-reported — unverified*. Counts use one vocabulary everywhere: **answers** (questions the founder answered) → **founder-reported claims** · **deck claims** · **UNKNOWN** · **contradictions** · **ledger items** (everything). **Skip** parks a question (it returns as an end-of-call follow-up) and moves on; an UNKNOWN answered with "unknown" no longer loops. No duplicate follow-ups.
- *Conviction / Founder Calls*: one page scroll — the detail pane no longer has its own clipped scroll box; the company list sticks below the toolbar and always fits the window. Every tab and control is reachable at 1280×720 … 1920×1080 and at 125 / 150 % zoom.
- *Send to Omar gate*: **Mark as sent** on a company whose IMQ state is not **IMQ — QUALIFIED NOW** asks for an explicit override reason (kept on the record as `omar_override`); cancelling leaves it unsent. Copying the text is always fine. Nothing is ever sent automatically.
- Robustness: a request the server cannot handle answers with a readable error instead of a hung page; malformed JSON gets a clear 400.

**Scout Loop Hardening RC1.1** (hardening only, no new features)
- Top bar (build label · your name · Taste Map · data stays local) is opaque, reserves its own height and never lets page content show through; the left navigation scrolls on its own when the window is short or zoomed, so every rail item stays reachable; the Conviction / Network toolbars grow with their content instead of spilling over the first rows.
- Duplicate safety: **+ Add company** for a name that already exists is refused with a pointer to the existing Conviction record (nothing merged, nothing overwritten); a second **Approve → Create** on an already approved Scout Intake item is refused; a second **End evaluation** returns the existing result instead of a second Founder Call. Founder rows typed as plain names are stored in the normal founder shape.
- IMQ: a person you add yourself in Network counts as identity-resolved (same rule as Founder Radar's operator default); a previously qualified company that loses a condition after a conflict spell shows **IMQ — STALE** with reasons, never a silent NOT YET IMQ.

**Scout Loop Hardening RC1**
- *Scout Intake scroll fix*: the toolbar (cycle · counts · search · Import Scout Packet) stays sticky and opaque; the page is the only scroll owner, so key unknown, evidence, provenance and history never slide underneath it (validated at 100 / 125 / 150 %).
- *Coverage intelligence* (Ecosystems page, top panel): for every ecosystem and signal family — founder formation, emerging companies, spinouts, grants, programs, events, general — an evidence-based state **HEALTHY / PARTIAL / GAP / UNKNOWN** computed from the configured sources and their recorded runs (configured · retrieved · productive · last successful retrieval). A successful retrieval with zero records from that ecosystem is PARTIAL, never HEALTHY; nothing scanned yet is UNKNOWN. Zero findings are labelled **ZERO FINDINGS — COVERAGE HEALTHY** or **ZERO FINDINGS — INSUFFICIENT COVERAGE**. Repeated signals from an unregistered place show **NEW ECOSYSTEM — COVERAGE REVIEW** (review only). The Chief Scout coverage audit (docs/coverage/) is loaded as *candidate watchers* with their access mode and limitations — a candidate never counts as coverage; **Adopt as watcher** (one explicit click per source) makes it a configured page watcher that stays UNKNOWN until a scan retrieves records. Extend ecosystems in `coverage_registry.json`, source declarations in `coverage_sources.json`, later audits in `coverage_candidates.json`.
- *Investment Manager Qualified* (Conviction detail, light-bulb block): a derived state on top of the existing Verified judgment — **NOT YET IMQ · IMQ — QUALIFIED NOW · CONFLICT GATED · IMQ — STALE** — with exact blockers: A verified score ≥ 80 (or the existing outlier route), B founder band ≥ 20 verified and venture-scale band ≥ 14, C founder identities resolved / no unresolved contradiction, D no unresolved portfolio conflict, E a verified, dated why-now trigger within 90 days (**Record dated why-now trigger** with a public source, or a confirmed dated company fact). Qualification is persisted (qualified_at, score, trigger, reason, evidence snapshot, conflict) and turns STALE when a material condition changes. It never sends anything — **Send to Omar** stays your click.
- *Active Evaluator* (Judgment rail): live diligence support for a founder conversation. Start from a Conviction company (**Start Active Evaluator**) or **New external evaluation**; paste a deck (statements become **DECK CLAIM — UNVERIFIED**); answer the proposed question in the founder's words (**FOUNDER-REPORTED — unverified**), type `unknown` for an UNKNOWN; challenges follow automatically (ARR definition, retention evidence, round timing, patents, pilots, 'no competition' vs known adjacent companies); contradictions are kept until you resolve them; evidence attaches to one claim only (**attach public source** / **operator verified**). **End evaluation** creates the Founder Call record, verification tasks (added to the company's verification checklist), UNKNOWNs, contradictions, follow-up questions, provisional notes and draft memo deltas — the Verified score is unchanged and nothing is sent to Omar. No audio, no recording.

**Scout Intake (Scout Integration v1)**
- Rail → *Sourcing → Scout Intake*: a staging inbox for Scout 00 / Chief Scout findings, stored in `scout_intake.json` in your data folder. *Import Scout Packet* takes a pasted JSON packet (`cycle_id`, `cycle_date`, `entities[]`, optional `events[]`, `summary`, `executive_brief`): it validates, previews (counts, duplicate warnings) and — only after your confirmation — stores every entity as **NEW INTAKE**. Import never creates a core Project Zero record.
- Statuses: NEW INTAKE · REVIEWING · APPROVED — CREATE · APPROVED — UPDATE · MONITOR · ARCHIVED · REJECTED. Unknown packet values stay **UNKNOWN** and are never read as negatives. Date discovered = packet `date_discovered` → `signal_date` → `cycle_date` → UNKNOWN.
- *Approve → Create* asks for the intended object type (Person / Founder Formation, Pre-company Opportunity, Company, Research Spinout = company or pre-company + `research spinout` tag) and runs the existing-entity check first (*Possible existing Project Zero record found* → Update existing / Create anyway / Cancel — never a silent merge). Records are created through the existing workflows only: a new scout-reported person enters exactly like an imported / radar-detected person (identity **needs identity resolution** — approving an intake item is never identity verification; resolve it in Founder Radar as usual), the existing pre-company opportunity behind the unchanged identity guard (while the identity is unresolved the opportunity is shown as PARTIAL / blocked with *Resolve identity before promotion*, the person link is kept, *Retry pre-company opportunity* after resolving the identity), the Conviction pipeline entry for companies (no score, no status change, freshness label unchanged). Every created / updated record carries `scout_provenance` (cycle, scouts, intake date, signal types, attention state, classifications, evidence URLs, key fact / unknown) — visible as a *Scout Intake — cycle …* chip on Conviction and in the person's sources / notes / history.
- Clean founder-formation flow (Human-Friction Reduction): **Confirm import** → **Approve → Create** (object type and the pre-company checkbox are prefilled from the packet; the person is created unresolved and every public packet URL is attached as candidate evidence *awaiting human confirmation*) → the **Review identity evidence** panel opens prefilled (person, company, source, claim, URL, status; nothing to retype) → **Confirm identity** (one click, recorded as your confirmation through the unchanged Founder Radar identity rule) → back on the same item: **Identity resolved — Create pre-company opportunity** (one click, existing promotion guard). *Identity evidence incomplete* means the packet lacks a public URL or a claim naming both person and company, or the Founder Radar rule refuses it — use the normal manual evidence workflow. The progress strip (Imported → Person created → Evidence attached → Identity confirmed → Opportunity ready → Opportunity created) is operational status only. Filter chip **Identity review ready** lists items waiting for your confirmation; nothing is ever confirmed in batch.
- Finding a scout-reported person later: the **Founder Radar** search box finds people by name across every bucket and matches company leads by claimed / scout-reported founder names (e.g. typing the founder's name shows the company lead and the unresolved person in every view).
- *Monitor* keeps the item here without a core record; *Archive* / *Reject* keep the item, its raw packet entry and history. Feedback fields (Mohamed outcome, Project A / Omar feedback, reason, next review date) are recorded for later scout-learning — no automatic learning.
- Cycle header: Scout 00's reported counts are kept verbatim (never rewritten) next to counts computed from intake statuses; the raw packet of every import is stored; the Executive Brief can be pasted per cycle. Scout 07 event recommendations enter Events only through *Add to Events* (Monitor / Ignore stay here); nothing is registered.
- Scout Bridge jobs can travel to Scout 00 and back automatically over the xAI API (your Scout 00 system prompt, replies only staged) — setup: [SCOUT00_TRANSPORT.md](SCOUT00_TRANSPORT.md). Grok-app Operating Cycle packets no longer need a paste: placed in `project-zero-data/scout00-inbox/` they are ingested into Scout Intake automatically (see [SCOUT00_TRANSPORT.md](SCOUT00_TRANSPORT.md#cycle-packets--the-scout-00-inbox)).
- Not built (by design): outreach, event registration, scoring from scout signals, ML feedback.

**Founder Calls (Founder Call Workspace Release)**
- Rail → *Founder Calls*: private notes of every conversation, Company → Founder → chronological calls, stored in `founder_calls.json` in your data folder. Start from *Log founder call* on a Conviction company, a Founder Radar lead or person, a Network person — or *Add external founder call* (name + company are enough; the founder gets a stable id and can be linked to an existing Project Zero record later without losing or duplicating calls).
- Templates: first scout call (six main questions, deeper follow-ups collapsed), follow-up / diligence, Project A introduction preparation. Unknown answers stay visibly **UNKNOWN**. After the call: a concise judgment (recommendation Pass / Monitor / Follow up / Consider for Omar, confidence Low / Medium / High, next action).
- Outputs: chronological history, due follow-ups (overdue / due today / due soon), a machine-generated summary from your own notes, an exportable Markdown call memo, and — only when you ask for it — an Omar / Project A briefing.
- Boundary: everything a founder says is **Founder-reported — unverified**. Call notes never verify identity or founder status, never change Gold/Strong, rankings or evidence gates, never create sourced facts, never trigger outreach or Network promotion. Public verification stays in the sourced-evidence workflow. No recording, transcription, scraping, calendar integration or automatic outreach.
