## 0. Executive summary (read this if nothing else)

Project A in 2026 is **not** a generalist "AI/fintech/climate" fund. Their money, their website, their newsletter and their newest deals all point at one underlying idea:

> **Software (increasingly AI agents) applied to Europe's physical, industrial, regulated and security-critical economy — sold B2B, entered at pre-seed/seed, in markets where regulation, fragmentation or sovereignty create structural moats.**

Their own five themes: **Defence Technologies · Industrial Autonomy · Regulated Industries · Frontier Technologies · Physical World Intelligence.** Fund V's stated focus: **defence, fintech, AI, supply-chain software**, at **€1–8M initial cheques, pre-seed & seed, 15–20 deals/year.**

What they are NOT doing anymore at venture stage: consumer apps, e-commerce, marketplaces without an industrial angle, horizontal "AI for everyone" tools. Their commerce/consumer history lives on in the **private-equity co-investment arm**, not in the venture fund.

**Consequence for Project Zero:** the Frankfurt/Darmstadt-first, "any tech-sounding GmbH" approach is misaligned. The right hunting grounds are Munich/Berlin/London/Nordics, industrial & defence ecosystems, and Europe-wide. Geography of the startup matters far less than pattern-fit.

## 3. THE TASTE MAP

> **Reading rule (revision 1):** Project A describes itself as investing *across sectors* with thematic depth ("combining deep thematic expertise with the breadth of a generalist platform" — their words). Everything below is therefore a set of **strong hunting hypotheses**, not a closed mandate. A company with no portfolio twin can still be a top candidate via the **Outlier / New Frontier route** (§5.3).

### 3.1 Core themes (their words) → what it means in practice
1. **Defence Technologies** → sensing, C2/data fusion, logistics, autonomy, training; software-defined; NATO/Europe.
2. **Industrial Autonomy** → manufacturing, robotics software, procurement, supply-chain digitisation, negotiation agents.
3. **Regulated Industries** → B2B fintech infra, insurance infra, compliance automation (medtech, carbon, tax), "regulated-by-design".
4. **Physical World Intelligence** → energy procurement/settlement, EV charging, geothermal/mining, industrial certification, bio-manufacturing.
5. **Frontier Technologies** → photonic chips, electric aviation — rare, sovereignty-framed, exceptional teams only.

### 3.2 Sub-sectors they repeatedly back
Freight/logistics software · industrial procurement & trade · insurance risk/underwriting · SME payments & financing · compliance automation · manufacturing AI · defence sensing & logistics · autonomous ground/air systems · energy procurement & EV infrastructure · AI-agent infrastructure (secondary).

### 3.3 Business-model patterns
- B2B (overwhelmingly), sold to mid-market/enterprise or governments.
- "Do the job, don't just assist" — AI agents that own a workflow end-to-end (Nexcade, Tabular, Pactum, 11x).
- Managed marketplaces/trade platforms **with** embedded financing or logistics (Andercore, Waypoint, Metycle) — never a pure listing site.
- Clearing/broker/"pipes" businesses in fragmented markets (Enapi, ReStabilise, Riva).
- Compliance-as-moat: the regulation IS the product (Rematiq, Atmen, Claimer).
- Hardware acceptable **only** with a software/fleet/data layer and a sovereignty story.

### 3.4 Technology patterns
Applied AI/agents on domain data · sensor fusion & edge autonomy · document/data extraction in paper-heavy flows · fine-tuned small models · robotics software (not new robots for their own sake, except dual-use) · real-time data infrastructure.

### 3.5 Founder characteristics [INFERENCE — verify with Omar]
- **Domain insider + technical co-founder** is the modal pair (industrial/defence/finance insider who has felt the pain + engineer).
- Technical PhDs appear in deeptech/energy (Hades: Dr. Werner; Avoltra: Dr. Canzaniello; Quantum: Dr. Kriegel).
- **Repeat founders** get pre-seed money fast (Flow AI/Flowrite; Vay's von der Ohe ex-Zoox; Quantum's Seibel).
- Studio-style: strong duos with a thesis but no company yet — Project A explicitly wants these.
- [HYPOTHESIS] They like founders who can sell into conservative buyers (ex-industry, ex-consulting industrial practices, ex-officers for defence).

### 3.6 Typical stage / traction at entry
Pre-seed & seed is the rule since Fund V; day-zero via Studio. Traction at entry ranges from pre-product (Hades, Scipio) to first pilots (Nettle, Nexcade). **They buy conviction in team + market structure, not revenue.** A company already at Series A is mostly too late (unless a "thoroughbred" exception).

### 3.7 Geography
Berlin, Munich, London dominate the current vintage; Nordics (Copenhagen/Stockholm/Helsinki) strong; Paris, Amsterdam, Vienna, Cologne present. **Munich = the defence/industrial/deeptech cluster** (Quantum, ARX, Linexa, Avoltra, Hades, Atmen, Vestigas, Vaeridion). **Frankfurt/Rhein-Main: effectively zero venture deals; Switzerland: one (42matters, 2011).**
→ Implication: Rhein-Main is a coverage gap for them, but also thin ground for their taste (its strengths — banking, insurance, Fraunhofer SIT security, hessian.AI — only partially overlap: insurance infra, cyber/risk, AI compliance).

### 3.8 Signals that make a company "unusually Project-A-like" (the 85% feel)
- European B2B company where the buyer today runs the process on email/PDF/Excel/phone, and the founder names the exact job title being replaced.
- A regulation (MiCA, MDR, CBAM, KRITIS, export control, tax) that everyone else sees as friction and the company treats as the product.
- Defence/resilience relevance with a **commercial market beyond defence** (dual-use) and integration into legacy systems.
- Fragmented cross-border physical supply chain (metals, chemicals, parts, freight) + embedded finance/logistics.
- Munich/Berlin/London + a domain insider + a technical co-founder.
- Team exploring pre-company with a clear thesis → Studio candidate.
- The company would visibly benefit from a 140-person ops platform: heavy enterprise GTM, hiring engineers, BI.

## 4. Sourcing archetypes (what to actively hunt) — hypotheses, not a mandate

Each archetype = a sentence you can test a company against, plus a portfolio twin **for pattern recognition only** (see conflict check). Treat the list as where the odds are highest, not as the only place a great company can come from. Archetype 15 is the deliberate escape hatch.

1. **Vertical AI agent for a paper-heavy operational job in a traditional European B2B industry** — freight forwarding, customs, insurance claims/underwriting, accounting/tax, construction logistics, medtech/pharma compliance, energy trading back-office — where the founder can name the role being replaced. *(Nexcade, Tabular, Nettle, Rematiq, Vestigas)*
2. **Cross-border industrial trade or procurement platform for a fragmented physical commodity** (metals, chemicals, fertiliser, packaging, industrial parts, building materials) **with embedded financing, logistics or pricing intelligence.** *(Andercore, Waypoint, Metycle, Laserhub)*
3. **"Regulated-by-design" financial infrastructure**: stablecoin/e-money issuance under MiCA/FCA, B2B payment rails with credit, SME working-capital in a vertical (construction, logistics), non-dilutive financing platforms. *(ReStabilise, Riva, Mimo, Copay, Re:cap)*
4. **Insurance infrastructure for new or under-served commercial risks** — MGAs, AI underwriting/inspection, cyber/climate/fleet risk data. *(Baobab, Nettle)*
5. **Defence software layer**: sensor fusion, C2/C4ISR integration, resupply/logistics planning, training & simulation, EW/data — software-defined, works with legacy kit, European/NATO customers, dual-use path. *(Project Q, Scipio)*
6. **Dual-use autonomous platforms with a fleet/software brain** (UGV, UAV, maritime, counter-drone) from teams with defence access **and** a commercial market (agriculture, infrastructure inspection, logistics). *(ARX, Quantum Systems, STARK)*
7. **Industrial autonomy software for manufacturing**: PLC/automation-logic AI, adaptive robot control, predictive maintenance, MRO/spare-parts optimisation, digital twins for retrofitting old plants. *(Linexa, Micropsi, Resourcly)*
8. **Energy & critical-infrastructure intelligence**: industrial energy procurement/PPA optimisation, EV-charging settlement/clearing, grid-asset software, geothermal/critical minerals with a sovereignty story. *(Avoltra, Enapi, Hades)*
9. **Decarbonisation compliance data infrastructure**: CBAM, product carbon certificates, Scope 3 in agri/steel/chemicals — where an EU regulation creates a mandatory buyer. *(Atmen, Root Global)*
10. **Sovereign compute / chips / photonics / secure infrastructure** — Frontier, only with world-class technical founders and an "Europe cannot depend on X" narrative. *(Black Semiconductor)*
11. **Agent-infrastructure with a sharp wedge from repeat/technical founders** (evaluation, fine-tuning, orchestration, domain-specific small models) — London/Nordics-heavy. *(Distil Labs, Restack, Flow AI)*
12. **Workforce & skills platforms for physical industries with labour shortages** (installers, technicians, defence/industrial hiring, workforce in hard regions). *(Montamo, Labrys)*
13. **Studio-style pre-company duos**: a domain insider + engineer with a thesis in archetypes 1–9, no company yet, ideally Munich/Berlin/London. *(11x, ARX, Enapi all came through the Studio)*
14. **Sovereignty-framed bio-manufacturing / materials** (bio-fermented ingredients, alternative feedstocks) — rare; needs exceptional team. *(Twig Bio)*
15. **OUTLIER / NEW FRONTIER (no portfolio twin required):** an exceptional founding team attacking a huge or newly-opened market with a structural "why now", in Europe, at pre-seed/seed — even if nothing in the portfolio rhymes with it. Scored through §5.3. This is where the next theme comes from; Project A's own history (Trade Republic in 2015, Quantum Systems' dual-use bet "before the market did") shows they take these.

Deliberately **not** archetypes: consumer, D2C, gaming, generic SaaS, horizontal SDR/CX agents (crowded; 11x/Dixa exist already), therapeutics.

## 5. Project A Fit Score /100 — revised model (v2)

### 5.1 Scoring blocks (revisions 3, 4, 5)
Portfolio resemblance is now a **minor** block. The score rewards founders, market size, why-now, genuine taste fit, earliness and insight.

| Block | Points | What it asks |
|---|---|---|
| **Founder signal** (human-verified only) | 0–25 | Domain insider + technical co-founder? Repeat founder / prior exit? Evidence they can sell to conservative buyers (industry, government)? Unknown team = 0 until you check. |
| **Venture-scale potential** *(new)* | 0–20 | Could this plausibly become a €1B+ company? Is the market huge or clearly expanding? Does the initial wedge expand into a much larger platform (workflow → system of record → payments/marketplace)? European → global path? **A perfect thematic match in a tiny market caps here at ~5.** |
| **Why now** *(new)* | 0–15 | Is there a structural reason this must exist NOW: AI capability step-change, new regulation (MiCA, MDR, CBAM, NIS2, defence procurement reform), labour shortage, geopolitical shift/re-armament, cost-curve crossing, new hardware capability? No credible why-now → max 3. |
| **Project A taste fit** | 0–15 | Does it sit in one of the five themes AND show the underlying taste (B2B, industrial/regulated/security-critical, moat from structure)? Sector buzzwords alone = 0. |
| **Earliness** | 0–10 | Pre-company/pre-seed with no institutional round = 10; seed = 6; Series A+ = 0. |
| **Differentiated insight** *(new)* | 0–10 | Does the team know something non-obvious about the buyer/market that a generalist wouldn't (proprietary distribution, data, regulatory access, hard-won operational insight)? |
| **Portfolio resemblance** | 0–5 | Rhymes with a portfolio company's *underlying taste* = up to 5. Pure cloning = 2. No twin = 0 — but that is **not a penalty** (see 5.3). |
| **Penalties** | −5 to −30 | Consumer (−20) · non-European without European founders (−15) · already known to every VC / Series A+ (−15) · AI-wrapper with no vertical or data wedge (−10) · **direct competitor to an active portfolio company (−25, see 5.2)** |

Sum caps at 100. **Interpretation: 80+ investigate immediately · 65–79 research this week · 50–64 watch · <50 drop.**

### 5.2 Portfolio-conflict check (revision 2) — mandatory before any "Keep"
For each candidate, classify its relationship to the active portfolio:
- **Similar taste, different market** → positive (+ up to 5 in resemblance). *Example: an AI agent for customs brokers vs. Nexcade (freight forwarders).*
- **Complementary** (would be a customer, supplier or integration partner of a portfolio company) → positive, and a concrete reason to contact — Project A's platform can make the intro.
- **Direct competitor** (same buyer, same job, same geography as an *active* portfolio company) → **−25 and a mandatory warning in the memo: "Potential portfolio conflict — do not forward until Omar/Project A confirms whether it is investable."** Whether two companies truly compete is nuanced (adjacent segments, different geographies, partnership potential); Project A makes that judgment, not the algorithm. *Example: a second AI commercial-insurance underwriting tool for the same European carriers would conflict with Nettle; a second defence resupply-planning tool would conflict with Scipio.*
- **Unclear** → mark "conflict: verify" and ask Omar before forwarding.
The tool must show this classification next to every portfolio twin it names. "Looks like Nettle" without the conflict line is forbidden.

### 5.3 Outlier / New Frontier route (revision 1, made genuinely open in v2.1)
This route exists to catch the next theme **before Project A has named it**. It is therefore deliberately NOT bound to the current thesis: an outlier may be non-industrial, non-regulated, non-defence, non-physical — consumer-adjacent, developer-facing, scientific, creative, anything. The only requirement is that the bar is extremely high on five things:
1. **Exceptional founders** — Founder block ≥ 22 (verified: repeat/exit, world-class technical, or clearly unusual track record).
2. **Huge venture-scale market** — Venture-scale ≥ 17 (credible €1B+ path, expanding market, global from Europe).
3. **Powerful structural why-now** — Why-now ≥ 12.
4. **Genuine technological/product differentiation** — Differentiated-insight block ≥ 8 (something hard, not a wrapper).
5. **Early enough** — Earliness ≥ 6 (pre-seed/seed, no large institutional round).
If all five hold, the Taste-fit block is scored on *European early-stage tech excellence* rather than theme membership (max 10 there), portfolio resemblance is ignored, and the memo opens with: **"OUTLIER — no portfolio twin; the emerging thesis is X."** Expect fewer than one true outlier per month; that is fine.
### 5.3b Three fields that must never be collapsed into one (v2.2)
| Field | Question it answers | Values |
|---|---|---|
| **DISCOVERY source** | Where did we find them? | pre-company channel (DIANA/EUDIS, TUM/UnternehmerTUM, transfer office, grant winner, stealth/talent exit) · announcement feed · register · manual |
| **EARLINESS** | How early are we relative to the market? | pre-company · grant/angel only · pre-seed closed · seed closed · Series A+ |
| **FUNDRAISING TIMING** | When is contacting commercially useful? | raising now (evidence) · likely 0–6 months · likely 6–12 months · recently funded / probably not raising · unknown |

Rule: **"pre-seed just closed" is NOT "seed is open."** Recent funding often *lowers* outreach urgency — the right move is congratulate, learn, stay close, and note the likely next-round window. Announcement feeds are **discovery** sources; the sourcing *edge* stays with the pre-company channels (row 1 of Discovery). Urgency is driven by Fundraising Timing, not by how fresh the announcement is.

### 5.3c Provisional vs Verified scores (v2.2)
Every company carries two numbers until research is done:
- **Provisional Project A Fit** — the full rubric with unverified blocks scored at face value from public coverage.
- **Verified Project A Fit** — the same rubric with unverified blocks zeroed: Founder block → 0 until founders' backgrounds are checked; Earliness → 0 until funding status is confirmed; Insight → halved until traction is confirmed.
The dashboard shows both, e.g. *Provisional 70 · Verified 42 (founders, funding unverified)*. An unknown team must never look as certain as a researched one. Only Verified scores are used to compose the weekly Top 10; Provisional scores only rank the research queue.

### 5.4 Weekly output rule (revision 7)
Every "PROJECT A RADAR — TOP 10" is composed as:
- **8 high-confidence** candidates (archetypes 1–14, scored ≥ 65),
- **1 emerging / adjacent-thesis** candidate (touches a theme from a new angle, or a sub-sector with no portfolio company yet),
- **1 genuine wildcard** (Outlier route: exceptional founder + market evidence, no twin required).
If a slot has no worthy candidate that week, the slot is left **empty and labelled** — never padded. Volume is not the goal; three genuinely Project-A-quality companies per month beats 300 tech-sounding GmbHs.
